Save a message without sending it.

## What you get

- A **Save draft…** entry in thread and new-thread composer plus menus, desktop send dropdowns, and mobile send-button long-press menus.
- A normal queued-message card that you can edit, delete, reorder, or **Send now**.

## How it works

1. Write a message in a thread or on the new-thread screen.
2. Open the plus menu or desktop send dropdown, or long-press the send button on mobile, and choose **Save draft…**.
3. Use **Send now** on the queued-message card when you are ready.

The plugin submits through the normal composer, preserving attachments, mentions, and the execution choices on screen. A saved new-thread draft preserves its selected provider, model, environment, and permissions until you send it.
