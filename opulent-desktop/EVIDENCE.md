## Naming correction

There is no `OpulentiaAI/comet`. "Comet" in the objective resolves to `Git-Godssoldier/comet`, a fork of
**`zeronsh/zeron`** (homepage `zeron.sh`). That is the shell/design reference. `OpulentiaAI/bb` exists and is a
fork of `get-bb/bb` (last pushed 2026-07-21).
