## Dictate a message

1. Click **Start voice input**, the microphone in the message box. On a phone, it replaces send while the box is empty.
2. Allow microphone access if asked.
3. Speak for at least 1 second.
4. Click **Stop and add to draft** to edit the text first, or **Send voice input** to send it.

**Cancel recording** throws it away.

## Choose the transcription service

Set **Voice input** in **Settings → AI services**:

| Choice | Transcribes with | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Automatic** | bb cloud, or another available service | Nothing more |
| **bb cloud** | bb's own service | Signing in to your bb account |
| **Codex** | OpenAI's transcription model, through Codex | [Codex](/docs/codex) set up on your primary machine, and that machine connected |

```sh
bb settings ai-services
bb settings ai-services set voice automatic
```

## Choose the microphone

Pick a **Microphone** under **Voice Input** in **Settings → General** (default **System default**), or right-click the microphone for **Voice preferences**.

## Transcribe an audio file

```sh
bb voice transcribe meeting.webm
bb voice transcribe call.mp3 --type audio/mpeg
```

Pass `--type` for files that aren't WebM.

## Check it worked

The box shows **Recording**, then **Transcribing**, then the text in your draft or as a sent message.

## Related troubleshooting

- "Voice input needs an HTTPS connection to this server": open bb over HTTPS or `localhost`, for example through [bb connect](/docs/set-up-remote-access#bb-connect).
- "Microphone permission denied": allow the microphone for bb in your browser or system settings.
- "Couldn't transcribe your recording": the recording stays under **Voice input failed**. **Retry**, **Download** or **Discard recording**.
- "Recording too short (minimum 1 second)": record for longer.
