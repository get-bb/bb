Use this when you'd rather speak a message than type it.

## Dictate a message

1. Click **Start voice input**, the microphone in the message box. On a phone, it takes the place of the send button while the box is empty.
2. Allow microphone access if your browser or system asks.
3. Speak. Recordings must be at least 1 second long.
4. Click **Stop and add to draft** to add the text to your message so you can edit it, or **Send voice input** to send it right away.

To throw a recording away, click **Cancel recording**.

## Choose the transcription service

Open **Settings → AI services** and set **Voice input**:

| Choice | Transcribes with | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Automatic** | bb cloud, or another available service | Nothing more |
| **bb cloud** | bb's own service | Signing in to your bb account |
| **Codex** | OpenAI's transcription model, through Codex | Codex set up on your primary machine, and that machine connected |

From a terminal, `bb settings ai-services` shows the current choice and `bb settings ai-services set` changes it.

## Choose the microphone

Open **Settings → General** and pick a **Microphone** under **Voice Input**. The default is **System default**. You can also right-click the microphone in the message box to open **Voice preferences**.

## Transcribe an audio file

An agent or script can transcribe a recording with the same service:

```sh
bb voice transcribe meeting.webm
```

Pass `--type` for files that aren't WebM, such as `--type audio/mpeg` for MP3.

## Check it worked

While you speak, the box shows **Recording**, then **Transcribing**. The text appears in your draft, or as a sent message.

## Related troubleshooting

- "Voice input needs an HTTPS connection to this server": browsers allow the microphone only over HTTPS or on `localhost`. Open bb over HTTPS, for example through bb connect.
- "Microphone permission denied": allow microphone access for bb in your browser or system settings.
- "Couldn't transcribe your recording": bb keeps the recording in the message box under **Voice input failed**. Click **Retry**, **Download** to save it, or **Discard recording**.
- "Recording too short (minimum 1 second)": hold the recording for longer.
