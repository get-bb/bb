## What to send when you ask for help

If nothing here matches, ask on bb's Discord or open an issue on GitHub, and include:

- Your bb version (`bb --version`) and operating system, and how you run bb: the desktop app, `npx bb-app`, or a service.
- The exact message, copied as text, and where it appeared.
- What you did just before, step by step.
- The server and host daemon logs from `~/.bb/logs/` from around that time. In the desktop app, open them with **View → Local Logs**.
